A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you
step inside.

What is the Nuttyinc Early Access Program?+

It’s a way to get early access to new NuttyVR projects, Mora, and upcoming features as development builds become available. You can try what’s next and share feedback before the public release.

What is Mora?+

Mora is an encryption app from Nuttyinc. The program offers an early look at the app as it develops. Features and release details will be shared with available builds.

Will I get access right away?+

Access depends on which projects and builds are ready. Submitting a request lets Nuttyinc know what you’re interested in; it doesn’t automatically unlock a download or guarantee a release date.

Do I need a VR headset?+

You’ll need a compatible headset to test NuttyVR projects. Mora and other features may have different requirements. Check the instructions for each build before installing it.

Where do I get builds and share feedback?+

Check the program’s releases and your access request for updates and instructions. Share bugs and ideas through GitHub feedback. Include the project, version, and what you experienced.
